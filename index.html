Honest feedback

A number, not a vibe.

Apple Vision tracks 19 body joints on the phone, thirty times a second. POZE compares limb angles against the reference, ignores where you stand and how tall you are, and shows one score you can act on.

  • Red to yellow: the big shapes are there
  • Yellow: fix the arms and the head
  • Green: hold it, shoot

Questions

Short answers.

Does POZE edit or generate photos?

No. POZE guides a real person into a real pose. The file in your library is exactly what the camera captured. You can optionally save a second copy with the guide drawn on it.

Do my photos leave the phone?

Never. Body tracking runs with Apple Vision on the device. The network is only used to download pose packs, for App Store purchases, and for gift-code unlocks.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in screen. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID and restore from the App Store.

Which iPhones are supported?

Any iPhone on iOS 17 or later. Newer phones track a little faster and offer more zoom presets.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. POZE is iPhone-first. Tell us you want it and we will count the votes.
